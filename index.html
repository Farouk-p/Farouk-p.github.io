01 — About

About Me

I'm a QA Automation Engineer with over 5 years of experience in the banking and fintech space. I hold a B.Eng. in Civil Engineering from the University of Ilorin, Nigeria — a background that sharpened my analytical thinking and problem-solving approach to quality assurance.

I specialize in designing and executing both manual and automated test strategies using tools like Cypress, Playwright, WebDriverIO, and Test Sigma. I'm experienced in API testing with Postman & Swagger, load/performance testing with JMeter, and CI/CD integration using Jenkins & GitHub Actions.

Throughout my career at Wema Bank, Mplify Limited, and Riby Finance, I've helped teams reduce defects, improve release confidence, and deliver quality software across web and mobile platforms.
